Production Fit

Building materials packaging must carry dense powder products while keeping a clean, professional bag appearance. Dry mortar, tile adhesive, putty powder, gypsum compounds, waterproofing mortar, and similar materials often require strong bags that can withstand filling, stacking, transport vibration, and job-site handling.

The application image reflects a packaging scene where different building material products need consistent bag shape, strong sealing, and clear surface presentation. LIGUOXING helps customers build production lines that can support multiple SKUs while keeping changeover practical and output stable.

Why It Fits

+

Stable forming helps keep bags upright and neat for retail, warehouse, and construction channels.

+

Strong sealing helps protect powdered materials from leakage, moisture, and rough handling.

+

Servo adjustment supports frequent size changes across dry mortar and additive products.

+

Consistent bag geometry improves downstream filling, stacking, and palletizing efficiency.

Packaging Requirements

For building materials, bag quality is judged by both performance and appearance. The package must protect the powder, reduce leakage during filling, stand squarely on pallets, and present product branding clearly. Many producers also need one production platform to handle several bag sizes and product categories without excessive downtime between batches.

LIGUOXING evaluates fabric type, lamination structure, bag width, bottom width, filling process, finished bag stacking method, and printing or surface requirements. This makes it easier to match the correct forming route, sealing strength, and optional downstream handling to the customer's real production environment.

Recommended Production Route

For dry mortar and construction powder packaging, the line can be configured with reliable material feeding, stable forming, hot-air sealing, accurate cutting, and finished bag collection. When paired with suitable printing or lamination equipment, the bag can achieve both industrial strength and a polished market-facing appearance.
