About LIGUOXING

Powerful Source Factory for Block Bottom Bag Making Machines

Backed by 30 years of rich production experience, Qingdao Liguoxing Precision Machinery Co., Ltd. is a powerful source factory specializing in block bottom valve bag making machines. Equipped with advanced CNC machining centers and laser cutting platforms, our profound in-house manufacturing capabilities empower our core product: the BVM-120 Machine. Continually innovated since its first generation in 2014, the current model features fully servo-driven control, 120 pcs/min high-speed production, top-tier hot air welding, and 64 needles/cm² nano micro-perforation for high-strength, dust-free packaging solutions. We provide premium production lines for cement, chemicals, building materials, and food products, strictly ensuring no defective products leave the facility. With over 200 sets delivered worldwide and rapid spare parts supply, Liguoxing is your trusted direct-factory leader in global industrial packaging
