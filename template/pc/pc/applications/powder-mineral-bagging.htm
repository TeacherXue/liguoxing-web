Chemical Powder Packaging

Micro-perforated industrial bags for fine chemicals and powder materials where air release, sealing reliability, and leakage control must work together.

Application 03

Production Fit

Chemical powder packaging is more demanding than ordinary bulk bag production because many materials are fine, fluid, dusty, or sensitive to contamination. Products such as titanium dioxide, resin powder, additives, mineral powder, and other industrial chemicals require bags that release air efficiently during filling while preventing product leakage after sealing.

The application image highlights clean, controlled powder filling where bag shape, valve performance, and sealing strength must remain consistent. LIGUOXING supports these requirements with bag making equipment designed for precise forming, hot-air welding, patch control, and nano micro-perforation.

Packaging Requirements

Fine chemical powders need a bag that breathes during filling but stays closed during storage and delivery. If air cannot escape, filling speed drops and bags may deform. If perforation or sealing is poorly controlled, powder leakage can contaminate the work area and damage the customer's brand. This is why chemical powder packaging depends on the balance between permeability, strength, and sealing accuracy.

LIGUOXING reviews particle size, powder flow behavior, filling equipment, required air release, target bag dimensions, and fabric lamination. The goal is to help customers choose a process configuration that protects product quality while maintaining efficient production output.

Recommended Production Route

For titanium dioxide, resin, chemical additives, and mineral powders, the recommended route combines controlled unwinding, accurate cutting, bottom and valve forming, hot-air welding, micro-perforation, counting, and automatic reject handling. This helps producers achieve clean bags, consistent size, lower leakage risk, and more predictable downstream filling performance.
