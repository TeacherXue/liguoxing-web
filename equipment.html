BVM-120 Model Block Bottom Valve Bag Making Machine

Equipment Function Configuration

Double Unwind Station

Cutting

Bottom Folding

Hot-air bottom welding with high strength and temperature compensation.

Finished Bags Delivery

Finished bag delivery section with automatic counting.
