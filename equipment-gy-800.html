Equipment Function Configuration

  • Double unwind station with auto roll exchanging without machine stop.
  • Overlapping printing process with options for different color configurations.
  • Printing roller and gear can be changed when printing length is adjusted.
  • +/-10 mm lateral and 360-degree longitudinal differential alignment without stopping for high precision printing.
  • Equipped with printing roller hoisting device for easier setup and maintenance.
  • Diaphragm pump auto ink supply for stable and continuous inking.
  • Intelligent air-drying convection system to ensure rapid ink drying on base fabric.
  • Double rewind station with auto roll exchanging and auto rewind rolls unloading.
